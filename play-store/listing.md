## App name (max 30)
Turflet: Book Turfs & Courts

## Short description (max 80)
Book turfs & courts near you. Build a squad, split the cost, pay by UPI.

## Full description (max 4000)
Turflet is the easiest way to book a turf or sports court near you, and get your whole squad on the pitch.

Find a box cricket turf, football pitch, pickleball or badminton court, see live slots, and book in a couple of taps. Then invite your friends, split the cost, and turn up to play.

FIND A PLACE TO PLAY
• Turfs and courts near you, sorted by distance
• Filter by sport: cricket, football, pickleball, badminton and more
• See photos, surface, courts, opening hours and price per hour before you book

PICK YOUR SLOT LIKE A MOVIE SEAT
• Live availability by date, court and time
• Choose a start time and how long you want to play (1 to 4 hours)
• Price shown upfront: no surprises at the venue

PLAY WITH YOUR SQUAD
• Create a group and tag friends who are on Turflet, or add guests
• Split the cost equally, or let the host cover everyone
• Need more players? Open your match to solo players nearby
• Or join someone else's match solo, no group needed

PAY SECURELY
• Pay by UPI, or use your Turflet balance
• Instant confirmation once payment goes through
• When friends pay their share, it goes back to the host's Turflet balance

SHOW UP & PLAY
• Every booking comes with a QR pass for quick check-in at the venue
• All your upcoming and past games in one place
• Match chat to sort out who's bringing the ball
